## Response

All three videos show **Microsoft HoloLens 2** hand interactions. I couldn't check literally every frame. Instead I took snapshots every few seconds through each video, looked closer at the moments where hands were in use, and checked what I saw against what the presenters say out loud. Video 3 has no speech, so for that one I read the on-screen tutorial prompts.

- **V1:** [The HoloLens 2 Tour](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rMks7sMzPxI) (hands-on software tour)
- **V2:** [HoloLens 2 On-Stage Live Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uIHPPtPBgHk)
- **V3:** [HoloLens 2 Getting Started](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AbH7CwLx_XE) (setup tutorial)

| Gesture used | What the gesture does |
|---|---|
| **Start gesture:** turn your palm up and tap the Windows icon on your inner wrist (V1 1:25, V3 3:00–3:40) | Opens the Start menu, where you find the time, battery, volume, apps, screenshot and record. It's also how you leave an app and go back to your mixed reality home. |
| **Direct touch / finger press:** reach out and poke with a fingertip (V2 4:00, V3 "Tap all gems" 0:18) | Presses holographic buttons, selects apps or tiles, and hits the Play/Stop buttons on holograms. With big buttons, people tended to press with the whole hand. |
| **Air tap:** pinch thumb and index finger together, then release, while aimed at something far away (V3 "Air tap all gems" 0:40, V1 2:23) | Selects or clicks things that are out of reach. In V1, a pinch also refreshes the scan of the room. |
| **Hand ray + pinch and hold:** a beam comes out of your palm, you pinch and move your hand (V1 4:30–4:45) | Grabs a far-away hologram so you can pull it closer or push it back into place without walking over. |
| **Grab and move:** pinch or grab a hologram up close, then move your hand (V1 3:04, V2 1:22, V3 "Move a flower" 1:10) | Picks up a hologram or app window and moves it anywhere in the room. |
| **Grab a corner of the box around a hologram and drag** (V2 1:22, V1 3:26) | Resizes the hologram (makes it bigger or smaller). |
| **Two-hand pinch:** grab with both hands and pull them apart or push them together (V2 1:38, V3 "Make the flower smaller" 1:50) | Scales the object up or down. |
| **Grab and twist your wrist, or drag the rotate handles** (V2 1:24, V3 "Rotate the flower" 2:10, V1 3:24) | Rotates the hologram. |
| **Pinch slider:** pinch the slider knob and drag (V2 3:30) | Changes a value smoothly (wind speed in the demo). It was the presenter's favorite because you feel your fingers touch. |
| **Touch slider:** stick your finger into the slider track (V2 3:09) | Jumps the slider straight to that spot. |
| **Push slider:** nudge the knob sideways, like an abacus (V2 3:19) | Moves the value left or right step by step. |
| **Finger scroll:** touch a list and drag (V1 2:56) | Scrolls through menus and lists, with a scroll sound as feedback. |
| **Ten-finger press:** play the virtual piano (V2 4:44) | Plays single notes or full chords, which shows that all ten fingers are tracked. |
| **Open palm held out** (V2 5:15) | A hologram (a hummingbird) flies to your hand, lands on it, and follows it around. |
| **Moving your hands in front of you** at sign-in (V2 0:37) | Lets the headset recognize your hands and adjust to your hand size. |
| **Grab and toss** in the Spatial app (V2 9:19) | Picks up an image and throws it onto a virtual wall, with physics. |
| **Clap** in the Spatial app (V1 9:45) | The presenter says clapping is special in Spatial, but the video doesn't show clearly what it triggers. |

**Not hand gestures, but also shown in V2:**

- **Looking at the bottom of a page** scrolls it automatically (eye tracking, 5:32).
- **Saying "Follow me"** makes a window follow you around (1:56).
- **Saying "Start dictation"** lets you type by voice (5:38).
